About Our Foundation

Green India Gokul Foundation is dedicated to building a greener, healthier, and self-reliant nation. Guided by ecological harmony and social compassion, we champion native tree afforestation, protect indigenous Desi cow breeds at our Goshalas, conduct rural healthcare and yoga wellness camps, and empower women and youth through sustainable community livelihoods.

🌱 About Green India Gokul Foundation

Rooted in Nature • Dedicated to Rural Prosperity

Swarna Bharathi Yoga & Gokul Trust Initiative

Green India Gokul Foundation was founded on the sacred principle that ecological preservation, animal compassion, and human well-being are inseparable. Working at the grassroots across Karnataka and rural India, our mission unites modern sustainable practices with time-honored traditional wisdom to build self-reliant rural ecosystems.

Our Five Core Pillars of Action:
🐮 Kamadhenu Cow Welfare & Sanctuary: Providing compassionate lifetime shelter, green fodder, and veterinary treatment to abandoned and rescued indigenous cattle, while training farmers in zero-budget natural farming and Panchagavya.
🌳 Community Afforestation: Planting native trees (Neem, Peepal, Banyan, Jamun) across barren village tracts, river basins, and school campuses with a committed 3-year nurturing cycle.
🧘 Yoga Awareness & Rural Healthcare: Conducting daily morning pranayama and asana sessions, alongside free medical diagnostic camps, eye checkups, and wellness education in underserved rural hamlets.
💼 Women Employment & Self-Help Groups: Supporting village women with tailoring cooperatives, organic product packaging, and vocational micro-enterprises to achieve financial independence and household prosperity.
🌿 Water Conservation & Sustainable Living: De-silting traditional village lakes and promoting rooftop rainwater harvesting, composting, and zero-chemical natural lifestyles.
Tax Exemption: All donations are 50% tax-exempt under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act.
